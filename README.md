# 🌾 FarmProof

### Smart Agriculture. Better Decisions. Stronger Farmers.

FarmProof is an agriculture-focused digital platform designed to help farmers access useful agricultural information, explore government schemes, understand market opportunities, and make more informed farming decisions through a unified web experience.

🔗 **Live Website:** [Visit FarmProof](https://ais-pre-wdmygpqbt4yh3qyjrdkspi-740344801215.asia-northeast1.run.app/)

## 🚀 Overview

Farmers often need to navigate multiple websites and sources to find agricultural resources, government benefits, and market information. FarmProof aims to simplify this process by bringing relevant farming-related information together in one accessible platform.

The project focuses on making agricultural information easier to discover and helping farmers connect with digital resources that support their day-to-day decisions.

## ✨ Key Features

- 🌱 **Agricultural Information:** Access useful farming-related information and resources.
- 🏛️ **Government Schemes:** Explore information about government initiatives and schemes relevant to farmers.
- 📍 **Map-Based Exploration:** Use map-based views to explore relevant agricultural information and locations.
- 📈 **Market Information:** Help users understand agricultural market trends and available price information.
- 🛒 **Agricultural Marketplace:** Support the vision of connecting agricultural sellers with potential buyers.
- 💻 **Accessible Web Experience:** Bring farming-related resources together in a single platform.

*Feature availability depends on the current implementation of the application.*

## 🎯 Our Mission

Our mission is to make agricultural information more accessible, reduce the effort required to discover relevant resources, and support a more connected agricultural ecosystem.

FarmProof aims to bridge the gap between farmers and digital agricultural services through technology-driven solutions.

## 🧩 How It Helps

FarmProof is designed around three key objectives:

1. **Discover:** Make agricultural information, government schemes, and resources easier to find.
2. **Understand:** Help users explore relevant information to support informed decisions.
3. **Connect:** Encourage better connections between farmers, agricultural resources, and market opportunities.
